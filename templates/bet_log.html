Gridline Bet Log

Every bet you place, graded two ways: whether it won, and whether you got a better number than the closing line. Beating the close consistently is the clearest early sign of a real edge.

Scoreboard

Closing line value: for spreads and totals, the points you gained on the closing number; for moneylines, the change in implied win chance. Positive means you bet a better number than the market settled on. For spreads, totals and moneylines the closing number fills in on its own after kickoff (the middle line across books at the last odds pull before the game); a number you type in always wins.
