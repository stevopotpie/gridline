How the live chance works

Live scores, possession, down and distance, field position and timeouts come from ESPN's public scoreboard, read by your browser every 30 seconds; the model runs right here on the page. Between plays it uses the last known situation. For a live spread or total, or to set any game state by hand, use the live panel on the calculator.
