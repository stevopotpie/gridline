Team profiles

Ranks are out of 32, from recent form weighted toward the last 8 games. Lower is better for defense stats and for seconds per snap. Punting and kicking rows are shown for reference; the model found they do not move scores. "2026 against Vegas" rows describe the season so far only. Game to game volatility combines swings in points, EPA per play and yards; ±x/±y is the typical swing in points scored and allowed. Neither volatility nor early results against the line have carried over in past seasons; since 2015, early-season results against the line have not carried over to the rest of the season. A repeat offender has lost by 17+ in 2 of its last 5 games or 3+ times this season. Since 2015 those teams got blown out again 19% of the time (others 11%), but Vegas lines already account for it: they covered 49%.

Overtime

Both teams are guaranteed a possession (regular season since 2025, playoffs since 2022), then it is sudden death. Regular season overtime is 10 minutes and can end in a tie; playoff overtime is 15 minute periods until someone wins.

Biggest needle movers

Points for a one standard deviation gap between teams (about the gap between an average team and a top 6 to 8 team), holding everything else equal. Factors are correlated, so QB rating and passing efficiency share credit.

Factors that did not help

Turnover margin, red zone TD rate, rushing efficiency, sack rates, bye weeks and short weeks, punter pinning inside the 10, net punting and kicker accuracy. Each made the projections slightly worse or no better on unseen seasons, usually because they swing week to week more than they repeat.

Backtest, 2018 to 2025

Each season was predicted by a model trained only on earlier seasons. "Before" is the PPG, weather, injury and returns model; "after" adds QB rating, success rate, passing efficiency, explosive plays, pace and penalties. Records count bets where the model and Vegas differed by at least the edge shown; pushes excluded. The added factors were picked by testing on these same seasons, so treat the "after" records as a best case.
