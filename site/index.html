Walk forward testing: each season was predicted by a model trained only on earlier seasons. The factors were chosen by testing on those same seasons, so these records are a best case, and Vegas is still more accurate on average. The 2026 season board grades every game as it happens.

How it works

Public play by play data from nflverse, about 540,000 plays since 2015, turned into pregame ratings: starting QB efficiency, scoring form, success rate, return units, workload from the previous game, wind and injuries to regular starters. Ridge regression projects the margin and total, and a second version starts from the Vegas number and learns what the market misses. Live sportsbook lines are pulled several times a week to compare against.

The pages rebuild automatically from the latest data. Projections are research, not betting advice, and sports betting is not legal in every state.
